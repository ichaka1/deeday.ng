How it works

How ordering a digital wedding invite works

You do not need an account, an app or any design skills. You choose a look, tell us about your day in one short form, and we build the invite and send you a link to share.

Step 1: Pick a design

Start on the design collection. Every design opens as a live sample, so you can try it on your phone exactly as your guests will. If you are not sure which one suits you, skip the choice and describe your style instead, for example "soft and romantic", "traditional Igbo" or "gold and burgundy". We will suggest a design.

Step 2: Fill one form

The order form asks for the essentials: your name and WhatsApp number, the couple's names, the date, venues, colours, your hashtag and any notes. When you submit, the form opens WhatsApp with your details already written out, so you only need to press send. We save a copy of your order too, so nothing gets lost if WhatsApp does not open.

After that we will confirm the plan, ask for your photos and share payment details. See current plans and prices for what each one includes.

Step 3: Get your invite

We build your invite and send you the link. Essential is delivered in 2 working days and Grand in 3, counted from when we have your details, photos and payment. You check it, ask for any changes within your revision rounds, and then share it.

Sharing it with guests

Your invite is a web link, so it works anywhere a link works: WhatsApp chats and groups, Instagram bios, text messages and email. Guests tap it and it opens in their browser. Nothing to download, and it does not lose quality when it is forwarded the way image flyers do.

What you will need to send us

  • The couple's names, as you want them shown
  • Dates, times and addresses for each event
  • Your colours or aso-ebi details
  • Photos, if your design uses them
  • Gift or account details, if you want a gift section

Questions about timing, changes or hosting are answered on our frequently asked questions page.

Ready when you are. Pick a design and tell us about your day.
