Yoruba wedding invitations

Digital invitations for Yoruba traditional weddings

A Yoruba traditional wedding (the engagement, or introduction and engagement) deserves an invite that carries the same richness as the day itself. We build digital invites with aso-oke inspired styling, your colours and every detail your guests need.

A design made for the occasion

Our Aso-Oke design uses wine, gold and aso-oke stripes for a rich traditional look. It is available on the Essential plan, so it is one of the most affordable ways to send a polished Yoruba traditional wedding invite. If you prefer a different feel, any design in the collection can be styled in your family's colours.

What to include for an engagement

  • The names of the couple and, if you wish, both families
  • Date, time and venue, with directions
  • Aso-ebi colours for each family and how to order
  • Whether the white wedding or reception follows, and when
  • Gift details, if the couple wants them shared

On the Grand plan, guests can order aso-ebi straight from the invite through WhatsApp, and you can keep the engagement and the white wedding on one wedding website with a schedule for each event.

Sharing with family

Yoruba weddings often have large family lists managed by parents and aunties. A single link makes that easier: everyone forwards the same up-to-date invite, and any change only needs to be made once. See how ordering works and the plans and prices.
