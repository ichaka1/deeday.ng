FAQ

Digital wedding invitation questions, answered

Straight answers to what couples ask us most. If your question is not here, message us on WhatsApp and we will reply.

Short version: see the plans and prices, or read how ordering works step by step.

What is a digital wedding invitation?

It is a wedding invite that lives at a web link instead of on paper or as an image. Guests open it on their phone and see your names, date, venues, countdown, RSVP, gift details and more.

How much does a digital wedding invite cost?

Essential costs ₦35,000 and Grand costs ₦100,000. Both include a year of hosting.

How fast can I get my invite?

Essential is delivered in 2 working days and Grand in 3 working days, counted from when we have your details, photos and payment.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. The invite opens in any phone or computer browser from a normal link.

Can I share it on WhatsApp?

Yes. The link can be sent in chats and groups, posted on Instagram or sent by text. Forwarding does not reduce the quality the way it does with image flyers.

Can guests RSVP?

Yes. On Essential, guests RSVP through WhatsApp. Grand adds RSVP keepsakes and a couple dashboard where you can see responses and wishes in one place.

Can I change the details after it is delivered?

Yes, within your revision rounds: 2 on Essential and 3 on Grand. Revisions cover wording and photo changes. Switching to a different design counts as a new build.

Can I use my own colours?

Yes. Tell us your colours or aso-ebi in the order form and we will style the design to match.

Can I get my own web address?

Every invite gets a yournames.deeday.ng link. Grand also includes your own domain, such as ujuemeka2026.com, for the first year.

How long will the invite stay online?

One year is included. Extra years cost ₦20,000 each. On Grand, your own domain renews at its yearly price.

Can guests order aso-ebi through the invite?

Yes, on Grand. Guests choose fabric options and the order goes to your aso-ebi contact on WhatsApp.

How do guests send gifts?

You can add your account details to a gift section with a one-tap copy button. Payments go straight to your account; deeday.ng never handles the money.

Do you make invites for traditional weddings?

Yes. We make invites for traditional, church, court and civil weddings, and for receptions and after parties.

How do I pay?

After you submit the order form, we confirm your plan on WhatsApp and send payment details. Work starts once payment and your details are in.
