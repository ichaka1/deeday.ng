Pricing

Digital wedding invitation prices

deeday.ng has two plans. You pay for the features you need, not for the design you pick, so every couple can choose the look they love and then the plan that fits their budget.

Essential

₦35,000

Clean, simple designs on your own yournames.deeday.ng link. Delivered in 2 working days.

Grand

₦100,000

Any design, animated openings, music, gallery and your own domain. Delivered in 3 working days.

Essential: ₦35,000

Essential is for couples who want a clean, beautiful invite that does the important jobs well. It works with our Essential designs, such as Church Wedding and Aso-Oke.

  • Your own link, for example yournames.deeday.ng
  • Your names, story and order of events
  • A live countdown to the day
  • Venue directions and an add-to-calendar button
  • RSVP through WhatsApp
  • A gift box with one-tap copy of your account details
  • Delivered in 2 working days, with 2 rounds of revisions
  • Hosted for 1 year

Grand: ₦100,000

Grand is the full wedding website. It includes everything in Essential, works with every design in the collection (including the animated looks like Emerald & Gold and Wine & Cream), and adds:

  • Your own domain, for example ujuemeka2026.com, with the first year included
  • A couple dashboard with RSVPs, guest list, wishes and aso-ebi orders in one place
  • An opening screen with background music
  • A photo slideshow and gallery of up to 25 photos
  • A love story timeline, schedule of events and dress code colours
  • Aso-ebi ordering through WhatsApp
  • RSVP keepsakes and guest wishes to the couple
  • A gift registry QR code
  • A PIN-protected private event, such as an after party
  • A guest photo wall for the day and personal reminder messages to your guests
  • Delivered in 3 working days, with 3 rounds of revisions
  • Hosted for 1 year

Delivery, revisions and hosting

Delivery is counted in working days from when we have your details, photos and payment. Revisions cover wording and photo changes. Switching to a different design after we have built yours counts as a new build.

Every invite stays online for a year. If you want it to stay up longer, for example as a keepsake for your anniversary, extra years cost ₦20,000 each. On Grand, extra years on your own domain are charged at that domain's yearly price.

Which plan should you choose?

If your main goal is to get the date, venue and RSVP to your guests in a way that looks good on every phone, Essential does that. If you want guests to open something that feels like an event in itself, with music, photos and a place for aso-ebi orders and gifts, Grand is the better fit. You can read how the ordering process works or check the answers to common questions before you decide.

Do I pay per design?

No. Pricing is by plan. Pick any design your plan covers, then the plan sets the features and the price.

How long does delivery take?

Essential is delivered in 2 working days and Grand in 3 working days, counted from when we have your details, photos and payment.

How long will my invite stay online?

One year is included with both plans. Extra years cost ₦20,000 each, and on Grand your own domain renews at the domain's yearly price.
