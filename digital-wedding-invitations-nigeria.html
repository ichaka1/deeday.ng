Digital wedding invitations in Nigeria: a practical guide

More Nigerian couples now send their wedding invitation as a link instead of a printed card or an image flyer. This guide covers what a good digital invite should include, how it compares with the alternatives, and how to roll it out to your guests.

Printed cards, WhatsApp flyers and digital invites

Most couples end up choosing between three formats, and each has a place.

Printed cards

Printed cards are still the most formal option and are well suited to parents, elders and VIP guests who expect a card in hand. The downsides are cost per copy, printing and delivery time, and the fact that a card cannot be updated if a venue or time changes.

WhatsApp image flyers

Flyers are quick and free to share, which is why they are everywhere. But an image gets compressed every time it is forwarded, small text becomes hard to read, and there is nowhere for guests to RSVP, find directions or see gift details without messaging you.

Digital invitation links

A digital invitation is a mobile web page made for your wedding. It shares as easily as a flyer, but it stays sharp, can be updated, and can hold far more: a countdown, maps, an RSVP form, aso-ebi details, music and photos. Many couples combine formats, for example a small run of printed cards for family elders and a digital link for everyone else.

What a good digital wedding invite should include

  • Your names, date and a clear schedule for each event (traditional, church or court, reception)
  • Venue addresses with a button that opens directions
  • An add-to-calendar button so guests do not forget the date
  • A simple way to RSVP
  • Dress code colours and aso-ebi details
  • Gift details with an easy way to copy account numbers
  • A countdown, photos and music if you want the invite to feel like an event

How to share it with guests

Send the link directly to close family first, then to your wider WhatsApp groups. Pin it in any wedding group chat, add it to your Instagram bio, and include it in reminder messages close to the date. Because the link always shows the latest details, you only need to correct something once.

For older guests who are not comfortable with links, a printed card or a phone call still works. The digital invite does not have to replace every other channel, it just needs to be the single place where the correct details live.
