Igbo wedding invitations

Digital invitations for Igbo traditional weddings

Igba nkwu, the Igbo traditional wedding, often brings together guests from the village, the cities and abroad. A digital invite gives all of them the same clear details in one link, styled in your colours.

Styled in your colours

Tell us your colours and the feel you want, for example ivory and forest green, burgundy and gold, or bold and modern, and we will style a design from the collection to match. Designs such as Ivory & Green, Emerald & Gold and Black & Gold work well for traditional celebrations with photos, music and a dramatic opening.

Details guests need for igba nkwu

  • The hometown venue with clear directions, since many guests travel in
  • Date and start time, plus any events on the days before or after
  • Aso-ebi colours and how to order
  • Where to stay nearby, for guests coming from other cities
  • The white wedding or reception details if they follow
  • Gift details, if the couple wants them shared

The Grand plan adds aso-ebi ordering through WhatsApp, a photo gallery and a couple dashboard to keep track of RSVPs and wishes.

One link for home and abroad

Guests in Lagos, Enugu, Onitsha, London or Houston all open the same link. If a time changes, you update it once and every guest sees the change. Read the practical guide to digital wedding invitations or compare the plans.
