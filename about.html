About

Made in Nigeria, for Nigerian weddings

deeday.ng designs and builds digital wedding invitations: one elegant link with your details, music, countdown, RSVP, aso-ebi and gift information, ready to share on WhatsApp.

Why deeday.ng exists

A Nigerian wedding is a production. There is the traditional, the church or court, the reception and often an after party, with guests spread across cities and countries. Coordinating all of that on an image flyer forwarded through family WhatsApp groups is how details get missed. Flyers get compressed, venues get mixed up, and the couple spends the week before the wedding answering the same questions again and again.

A digital invitation fixes that. It works like a small website: the details are always current, the map opens directions, the countdown runs live, and guests can RSVP from the same link. It reflects the size and beauty of Nigerian celebrations instead of squeezing them into a template made for a different market.

What we make

We build each invite for you. You pick a design from the collection, tell us your details, and we personalise it with your names, story, colours and photos. You get a live link, not a file. It works on any phone, needs no app, and stays online for a year. Read what each plan includes.

Our designs range from formal and scriptural, like Church Wedding, to traditional Yoruba styling in Aso-Oke, to full wedding websites with music and galleries such as Wine & Cream and Navy Editorial.

How we work

Orders come in through our website form and continue on WhatsApp, where we confirm details, collect photos and share progress. Every order is saved securely so nothing is lost between messages. We do not handle wedding gift money: gift details on your invite point straight to your own account.
