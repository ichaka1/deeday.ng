Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

deeday.ng (“we”, “us”) designs and delivers digital wedding invitations for couples in Nigeria and beyond. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • When you place an order or contact us: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, and the wedding details you choose to share (names, dates, venues, colours, story, bank/gift details, and any photos you send us). These order submissions are saved to our secure database so we can respond to you and keep a record of your order.
  • When your guests use an invitation we build: details they enter (such as RSVP name, attendance, meal choice and messages) may be stored in our secure database and made available to you, the couple, and/or delivered to you via WhatsApp. We use these responses to give you your guest list and, where you have opted in, to support wedding-related updates. We do not sell or repurpose your guests’ responses.
  • Basic usage data: standard, non-identifying analytics (such as page visits) that help us improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To design, build and deliver your invitation.
  • To contact you about your order and respond to your enquiries.
  • To improve our templates and service.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We communicate with you through WhatsApp and email, which are operated by third parties under their own policies. Our website is hosted by Vercel and our database by Supabase; these providers process data on our behalf as service providers, under their own security and privacy terms, and our database is hosted within the European Union. Gift payments are made directly to the account details a couple provides; we do not collect or store card or banking credentials.

Keeping your information

We keep order details for as long as needed to deliver your invitation and for reasonable record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have given us by contacting us using the details below.
