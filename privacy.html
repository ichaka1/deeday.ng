Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

deeday.ng (“we”, “us”) designs and delivers digital wedding invitations for couples in Nigeria and beyond. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • When you place an order or contact us: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, and the wedding details you choose to share (names, dates, venues, colours, story, bank/gift details, and any photos you send us). These order submissions are saved to our secure database so we can respond to you and keep a record of your order.
  • When your guests use an invitation we build: details they enter (such as RSVP name, attendance, meal choice and messages) may be stored in our secure database and made available to you, the couple, and/or delivered to you via WhatsApp. We use these responses to give you your guest list and, where you have opted in, to support wedding-related updates. We do not sell or repurpose your guests’ responses.
  • Usage data (only if you accept cookies): if you click Accept on our cookie notice, Google Analytics records how the site is used, such as pages visited, time on page, device and browser type, approximate location (city or country) and how you found us. This helps us see which pages are useful. If you click No thanks, Google Analytics does not load.

How we use your information

  • To design, build and deliver your invitation.
  • To contact you about your order and respond to your enquiries.
  • If you give us your email address on the order form, to send you a copy of your order. This is part of handling your order, so it does not need a separate consent.
  • Only if you tick the optional box on the order form, to send you wedding planning tips and occasional offers by email. Ticking it is never a condition of ordering. We record the wording you agreed to and when, and every such email has an unsubscribe link. You can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing or contacting us.
  • To improve our templates and service.

Cookies and Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use deeday.ng. It only runs after you accept it on our cookie notice. When it runs, it places cookies (such as _ga) in your browser and sends usage information to Google, which may process it on servers outside Nigeria. We do not use advertising cookies, we have switched off ad personalisation signals, and we do not use Analytics to identify you personally.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also block Google Analytics on every website with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

We also save a few small settings in your browser's local storage so the site works the way you left it: your light or dark theme and your cookie choice. These stay on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We communicate with you through WhatsApp and email, which are operated by third parties under their own policies. Our website is hosted by Vercel, our database by Supabase, spam protection on the order form by Cloudflare Turnstile (which checks your browser to tell people from bots) and, if you accept cookies, site analytics by Google; these providers process data on our behalf as service providers, under their own security and privacy terms, and our database is hosted within the European Union. Gift payments are made directly to the account details a couple provides; we do not collect or store card or banking credentials.

Keeping your information

We keep order details for as long as needed to deliver your invitation and for reasonable record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.
